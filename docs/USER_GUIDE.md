# User Guide: Flow for Windows

Welcome to **Flow for Windows**, your personal companion for distraction-free deep work, focused study, and mindful pacing.

---

## 1. Getting Started

When you launch Flow, you are greeted by the **Timer View**—a distraction-free circular countdown with smooth animated progress ring.

```
       [ Deep Work Focus ]
            25 : 00
           [ ▶ Start ]
          ● ○ ○ ○ (1/4)
```

### Basic Controls:
- **▶ Start / ⏸ Pause**: Starts or pauses the active timer. (Shortcut: `Space`)
- **↺ Reset**: Restores the timer back to the beginning of the current interval. (Shortcut: `R`)
- **⏭ Skip**: Advances directly to the next interval (e.g. Focus $\to$ Short Break). (Shortcut: `S`)

### Understanding the Cycle Indicator:
Under the timer controls, four small dots track your progress through the current Pomodoro cycle:
- **● ○ ○ ○**: Session 1 of 4.
- After completing 4 focus sessions, Flow automatically schedules a **Long Break** (default 15 minutes) to give your mind a well-deserved rest.

---

## 2. Using the Floating Mini Timer

When working in full-screen IDEs, Microsoft Word, or Adobe Suite, you may not want the large main application window visible.

### Opening the Mini Timer:
- Click the **Mini Timer** button in the top header or right-click the System Tray icon and select **Open Mini Timer**.
- The mini timer floats on top of all windows (`Always-on-Top`).
- Drag it by clicking anywhere on its background to position it in the corner of your screen. Flow remembers this position between sessions.

### Layout Options:
- **Compact View**: Displays just the numerical time and circular ring.
- **Expanded View**: Expands to display the session name, tag, and quick controls for Pause and Skip.

---

## 3. Managing Tasks & Linking to Focus Sessions

Instead of wondering what to work on next, manage your backlog directly inside Flow:

1. Click **Tasks** in the sidebar.
2. Enter a task title, select a priority (*High*, *Medium*, or *Low*), and estimate the required minutes.
3. Click **Add Task**.
4. Click the **Focus** button next to any task. Flow will automatically:
   - Switch to the main timer.
   - Set the session header to your task name.
   - Log completed focus intervals directly under that task.

---

## 4. Using the Study Planner

Prepare for exams, certifications, or major project milestones:

1. Open the **Study Planner** page.
2. Enter the subject name, target study hours, and pick the upcoming exam date.
3. The dashboard calculates the exact countdown (days remaining) until the exam.
4. Click **Study** on any subject to switch immediately to the timer with that subject active.
5. As you complete focus sessions tagged with that subject, the progress bar advances toward your goal.

---

## 5. Reviewing Analytics & Reports

Flow automatically compiles your daily productivity logs into actionable insights:

1. Click **Statistics** to view:
   - **Total Focus Time**: Cumulative minutes and hours logged.
   - **Daily Streak**: Consecutive days with at least one completed focus session.
   - **Activity Bar Chart**: Visual comparison of focus time across the last 7 days.
   - **Tag Breakdown**: Percentage of time spent on Deep Work, Study, Writing, etc.
2. In Settings, click:
   - **Export Sessions (CSV)**: Generates a standard CSV file with complete session timestamps and durations.
   - **Export Report (HTML)**: Generates a polished, printable HTML productivity report summary.

---

## 6. Productivity Insights & Goals

Click **Insights** in the sidebar to review your behavioral trends:
- **Prime Focus Window**: Discovers whether your highest concentration occurs in the morning (6am–12pm), afternoon (12pm–5pm), or evening (5pm–10pm).
- **Productivity Goals**: Track daily minutes and session targets with progress percentage indicators.

---

## 7. Customizing Settings

Access **Settings** from the bottom of the navigation menu:
- **Timer Durations**: Adjust Focus, Short Break, and Long Break durations.
- **Commitment Mode**: Select between *Off*, *Gentle*, *Strict*, and *Lockdown* to avoid premature skipping or resetting during focus sessions.
- **Auto-Start**: Configure whether breaks or focus sessions start automatically.
- **Sound Alerts**: Choose between *Synthesized Bell*, *Ambient Chime*, *Digital Beep*, or *Mute*, and test the sound with the **Preview** buttons.
- **Database Backup & Restore**: Export a full database backup or restore from a previous backup.
